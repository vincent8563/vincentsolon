# Taste-Skill Guidelines

## Typography
- Use distinctive, readable fonts (avoid Inter/Arial default)
- Strong hierarchy: H1 (4xl-7xl), H2 (2xl-3xl), body (base-lg)
- Tight letter-spacing on headings, relaxed on body

## Layout & Spacing
- Generous whitespace (padding/margin ≥ 1.5rem)
- Asymmetric grids & broken layouts where appropriate
- Max-width containers (prose or 4xl-6xl) for readability

## Motion & Interaction
- Subtle hover states (scale, color shift, underline)
- Smooth scroll & fade-in animations
- No heavy animation libraries (use Tailwind/CSS transitions only)

## Anti-Slop Rules
✅ DO: Unique layouts, intentional contrast, custom icons/SVGs, cohesive palette
❌ DON'T: Purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism overload, generic "bento" cards, stock AI imagery
